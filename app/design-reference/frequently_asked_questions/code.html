How long does printing take? expand_more

Standard digital and offset print runs (Business Cards, Flyers, Brochures) are ready within 24 to 48 hours at our Duplication Road, Colombo 03 production facility. Large format pull-up banners and custom die-cut packaging take 3–5 business days. Same-day express dispatch is available for orders placed before 10:00 AM within Colombo 01–15 suburbs.

Can I cancel or change my order? expand_more

Modifications or cancellations are free within 1 hour of job placement. Once automated preflight file checks pass and plate creation starts at Duplication Road, physical material consumption begins and production cannot be stopped without a 30% prepress processing recovery fee.

What file formats do you accept? Vector PDF, high-res TIFF, PSD, AI, JPG (min 300 DPI)
expand_more

We recommend single-page print-ready PDF/X-1a files with all fonts converted to outlines. Provide 3mm external bleed on all perimeter sides and maintain CMYK color profiles to ensure zero color shift between screen proofs and press output.

Do you offer island-wide delivery in Sri Lanka? expand_more

Yes! We ship to all 25 districts across Sri Lanka via our dedicated logistics partners (Pronto, Prompt Xpress, and Domex). Greater Colombo deliveries arrive within 24 hours of dispatch, while outstation orders (Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala, Jaffna) take 48–72 hours. Real-time SMS tracking updates are triggered upon dispatch.

How do I track my live order? expand_more

Open the Orders tab from your main navigation bar to monitor each job through 5 granular milestones: Artwork Preflight > Plate Staging > In-Press Running > Post-Press Finishing > Out for Dispatch. You can also opt-in for instant WhatsApp stage notifications.
