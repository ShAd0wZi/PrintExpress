Commercial Pre-Press Specifications

Ensure your artwork meets Sri Lanka industrial offset and digital printing tolerances for crisp, color-accurate output.

We accept print-ready vector formats for ultra-sharp typography and line art.

  • PDF/X-1a is the preferred exchange file standard.
  • Flatten transparencies or submit native Adobe Illustrator files.
  • Bitmap images must adhere strictly to 300-600 effective PPI.

Digital screens display in additive RGB, while physical presses print in subtractive CMYK.

warning

Convert all RGB assets to CMYK Fogra39 before upload to avoid color shifts.

Prevent missing font errors at prepress rip:

  • Convert all text to Outlines / Curves (Ctrl+Shift+O / Cmd+Shift+O).
  • Minimum legibility threshold: 6pt for regular sans-serif, 8pt for serif & reverse light text.
  • For small reverse text on rich black, apply a 0.15mm stroke to combat ink bleed.
